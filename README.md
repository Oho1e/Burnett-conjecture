# Burnett-conjecture

[简体中文](README.zh-CN.md) · [Contributors](CONTRIBUTORS.md)

This is the Lean development for **High-frequency vacuum limits with bounded first derivatives: a variational approach to the forward Burnett conjecture**. [GitHub Actions](https://github.com/Oho1e/Burnett-conjecture/actions)

The project is in `development`. This candidate contains 525 registered targets, 590 inventory entries, and 242 Lean sources. The F04 local results identify the weak Ricci limit and prove strong H⁻¹ convergence of the actual linearized Ricci in each preferred chart under explicit coordinate vacuum. The intrinsic-curvature results identify the chart Ricci trace and derive that coordinate-vacuum premise from the same intrinsic vacuum sequence. The Ward batch adds eight supporting endpoints for the finite-dimensional variation, Fourier Hessian, and gauge cancellation. The full Lorentzian data, global tensor tests, F05, and the transport theorem remain open. [Ward statements](docs/statements/transport-ward.en.md) · [Weak Ricci](docs/statements/weak-ricci-identification.en.md) · [Local strong H⁻¹](docs/statements/geometric-Hminus-closure.en.md) · [Intrinsic curvature](docs/statements/intrinsic-curvature-Ricci.en.md) · [Scope](docs/formalization/scope.json)

Four supporting results transport a given angular kernel through measurable coordinates and nonnegative weights. [Angular kernel transforms](docs/statements/angular-kernel-transforms.en.md)

The required scope includes the rotating null moment obstruction to transport representation (X03) and the multiscale Rosen vacuum sequence with exact zero-source coordinates (X06). X03 entered `main` with PR #13. This delivery implements X06, including the absence of a common derivative scale and the Einstein moment and transport identities in both coordinate systems. Author confirmations are tracked in the scope. [X06 statement](docs/statements/rosen-multiscale.en.md) · [Slice overview](docs/formalization/slices.en.md)

The flat-limit result X07 proves vanishing of the full intrinsic Riemann curvature for the smooth limit of an admissible flat sequence. [X07 statement](docs/statements/flat-rigidity.en.md)

The general two-polarization Rosen theorem (X05) constructs a full kinetic representation under the paper's physical first-derivative bound. The same graph measure gives the Einstein tensor moment, the Hamiltonian transport identity, and finite local second moments. [X05 statement](docs/statements/rosen-two-polarization.en.md) · [Mathematical review](docs/statements/rosen-two-polarization-review.en.md)

The F05 cutoff commutator for a fixed homogeneous angular Fourier multiplier tends strongly to zero on localized weakly null inputs. The result also covers fixed low-frequency regularization. [Angular Fourier commutators and fixed low-frequency regularization](docs/statements/angular-cutoff-commutator.en.md)
F05 represents a positive matrix of continuous-test functionals on a compact space by ordered complex measures dominated by one finite trace. [Positive matrix-measure representation](docs/statements/positive-matrix-measure.en.md)

The harmonic-coordinate example X01 proves divergence of the tensor wave-source L² norms on the fixed compact box. Each metric is intrinsically flat and the sequence satisfies the paper's admissibility bounds. [X01 statement](docs/statements/harmonic-shear.en.md)

X04 gives an actual smooth Lorentzian metric with positive null moments and no positive transported null-ray representation. [X04 statement](docs/statements/bianchi-counterexample.en.md)
The F08 support lemmas determine finite local measures by smooth tests and disintegrate a finite joint measure with a supplied bounded marginal density. [Local disintegration](docs/statements/local-disintegration.en.md)
