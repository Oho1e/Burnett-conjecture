# Actual bilinear and scalar Fourier multipliers

[简体中文](bilinear-fourier-operators.zh-CN.md)

These statements develop the physical operators used in the Fourier estimate at manuscript lines 660–677. All physical variables are four-dimensional. The tested actual bilinear output for Schwartz inputs has the negative output frequency \(-(\eta+\xi)\) in the third Fourier factor. The conversion to the manuscript's radian frequency uses \(\tau=2\pi\).

- For finite \(p,s,r>1\) with \(r^{-1}=p^{-1}+s^{-1}\), the actual bilinear output on Schwartz inputs of a real joint symbol that is smooth off zero and homogeneous of degree zero under positive dilations belongs to \(L^r\). A common bound \(D\) for the ambient Fréchet derivatives of orders 0 through 15 at the joint unit sphere in the fixed eight-dimensional coordinates gives \(\|T_m(f,g)\|_r\le K D\|f\|_p\|g\|_s\). The constant \(K\) is chosen before the symbol and inputs.
- The spectral-input version applies to two \(L^1\) spectra which are smooth off zero and whose inverse transforms lie in \(L^p,L^s\). The same expanding annular approximations converge in Fourier \(L^1\) and in the respective physical norms.
- For Schwartz inputs and a real measurable bounded joint symbol, a real measurable bounded output symbol in its bounded-multiplier realization yields an \(L^2\) output agreeing almost everywhere with the actual joint integral with that symbol evaluated at \(\eta+\xi\). The combined integral also has an \(L^r\) bound on the spectral inputs just described when both real symbols are smooth off zero and homogeneous of degree zero under positive dilations.
- Each real smooth-off-zero degree-zero scalar symbol has a bounded realization on every finite \(L^p\) with \(p>1\), agreeing with the Fourier \(L^2\) multiplier on their common domain and with the radian action on Schwartz inputs. The radial and angular factors give \(L^1\) spectra, smooth off zero, with inverse transforms in these finite physical \(L^p\) spaces.
