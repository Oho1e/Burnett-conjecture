# Complete cubic energy in the Lean library

[简体中文](complete-cubic-energy.zh-CN.md)

The proofs follow manuscript lines 601–729, using the same definitions as the actual bilinear Fourier estimates. The applications at lines 730–734 belong to the freezing and transport slices.

- For finite \(p,s,r>1\) with \(r^{-1}=p^{-1}+s^{-1}\), the actual commutator with a Schwartz coefficient \(f\) applied to a Schwartz input \(g\) is in \(L^r\). The real symbol is smooth off zero and positively homogeneous of degree one. A common bound \(D\) for its ambient derivatives of orders 0 through 16 on the four-dimensional unit sphere gives the bound \(KD\|Df\|_p\|g\|_s\) with \(K\) independent of \(b,D,f,g\). Here \(Df\) is the real Fréchet derivative of the complex-valued coefficient. The frequency convention is the one used in the manuscript.
- The fixed-symbol homogeneous continuity theorem estimates the difference of the two complete cubic energies using the \(L^3\) norm of the Euclidean gradient array of \(u-v\) and the squared sum of the two gradient norms. It uses the actual quasilinear second-derivative and constant-coefficient quadratic first-derivative terms and the stated transpose symmetry. The library also gives a quantitative \(KD\) version. The homogeneous polarization estimate uses the actual symmetric trilinear map and the product of its three gradient norms. The fields are real Schwartz fields in fixed orthonormal coordinates.
- For fixed real Schwartz profiles \(\chi,d\), the regularized symmetric trilinear map has the actual diagonal for the symbol \((1-\chi)b+d\). With the profiles and coefficient arrays fixed, the bound has constant \(K(D+1)\) and the product of the three Euclidean \(W^{1,3}\) norms. Each norm is the sum of the \(L^3\) norms of the value and full gradient arrays.
- The fixed-regularization continuity theorem treats a prescribed smooth real symbol agreeing with the homogeneous symbol outside a ball. For a fixed real cutoff that equals one near zero and vanishes outside a ball, the proof constructs the smooth compactly supported remainder. It estimates the difference of the actual energies in the prescribed symbol, with the \(W^{1,3}\) norm of \(u-v\) and the squared sum of the two \(W^{1,3}\) norms. The constant is fixed for that regularization before choosing the fields.
- For smooth real \(f\) with finite \(L^\infty\) norms of \(f\) and its full gradient, and smooth real compactly supported \(H\), the mixed quasilinear estimate bounds the absolute value of the imaginary part of the sum of the two ordered pairings by \(KD\) times \(\bigl(\|f\|_{\infty}\|\partial H\|_3+\|\partial f\|_{\infty}\|H\|_3\bigr)\|\partial u\|_3\|\partial v\|_3\). At \(u=v\) the mixed expression is the weighted quasilinear energy in the manuscript.
