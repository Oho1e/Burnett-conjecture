# Global intrinsic amplitude covariance and the original physical measure

For any strict original vacuum subsequence and a fixed auxiliary Riemannian metric r, one strict refinement supplies the original chart witnesses and a positive, regular control measure τ on the actual r-unit cosphere. The measure is finite over compact subsets of the base. A τ-measurable complex four-linear amplitude on the real tangent fibers has a positive semidefinite covariance matrix, unit auxiliary trace and zero intrinsic Ricci principal symbol almost everywhere. Its ordered complex entry measures recover the original chart amplitudes. On compact windows of compatible atlas charts their Borel change of variables uses the actual cotangent phase transition and the two Jacobian factors on each ordered pair.

On the null set, the real quarter contraction against the inverse background metric recovers the original positive physical measure. Its support, scalar curvature identity and all compactly supported smooth symmetric Einstein tensor moments are those of the same refined subsequence. With two auxiliary metrics on a nonempty base, keep the original subsequence and chart witnesses. If F is radial change of section, α is the squared receiving norm and t is the receiving trace of the original amplitude, the control changes by F₍*₎(αtτ) and the amplitude pulled back by F is divided by t almost everywhere. The physical measure changes by F₍*₎(αμ). The single-metric result also covers the empty base.

The local pairing uses the original volume half-density gradients, two independent smooth cutoffs on a compact represented chart window, and a supplied bounded L² operator with an actual local left Fourier representation and compact remainder. Its real degree-zero principal symbol gives the paper-oriented limit with the same global amplitude restricted to that window. A smooth compact-base order −1 symbol satisfying the stated derivative bounds gives a compact remainder. A finite represented plateau cover is available in a receiving chart. Establishing the actual classical pairing for arbitrary receiving charts and assembling the global F05 result remain open, as do the paper-level quadratic theorem and transport. This support delivery has no recorded author semantic confirmation.
