# Quantitative joint Fourier multiplier estimates

[简体中文](joint-fourier-multiplier-estimates.zh-CN.md)

These statements supply the trilinear joint multiplier estimate used at manuscript lines 660–671. The symbol \(m\) is real, smooth off zero, and homogeneous of degree zero under positive dilations on the joint frequency space \(\mathbb R^4\times\mathbb R^4\). The three complex inputs are Schwartz functions on \(\mathbb R^4\). The third Fourier test is evaluated at \(-(\eta+\xi)\).

- The tested physical dyadic block sums on scales \(2^{i-N}\), \(0\le i<2N\), converge to the actual joint Fourier integral as both ends of the truncation are removed.
- For \(p,s,z\) in \((1,\infty)\) with \(1/p+1/s+1/z=1\), the absolute trilinear pairing is bounded by the product of the three \(L^p,L^s,L^z\) norms times a linear combination of the actual reference kernel moments with weights \((j,k)=(1,1),(5,1),(1,5)\). The coefficients are finite and depend on the exponents and fixed windows.
- With a common bound \(D\ge0\) on ambient Fréchet derivatives of orders 0 through 15 on the Euclidean joint unit sphere, the pairing has bound \(K D\|f\|_p\|g\|_s\|h\|_z\). The fixed linear coordinate splitting defines the sphere seminorm. \(K\) is chosen before \(m,D\) and all input fields.
